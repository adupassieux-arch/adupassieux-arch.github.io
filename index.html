About

A career in international finance, read through an analyst's lens.

I am an independent strategic advisor working where finance, commodities and cross-border business meet.

For more than fifteen years I have worked across international finance, FX and commodity markets, and cross-border transactions — analysing markets, structuring positions and coordinating counterparties across jurisdictions. I founded and led MyCityLofts (Paris, Brussels, London) from 2012 to 2023, and served as Director of Commodities & Private Banking Advisory at AD Invest Ltd in London from 2017 to 2024. Alongside this, I have advised on the valuation and positioning of prestige residential assets.

My training covers both sides of the analysis: a BSc in Chemistry and a Master's in International Management. Data is a natural extension of that background, not a change of course — today I build models, dashboards and quantitative analyses in Python, SQL and Power BI to support the decisions my clients make.
