JPM Coin: Institutional Digital Deposits, Explained

A digital US dollar bank deposit token issued by JPMorgan Chase for instant settlement between vetted institutional clients: not a new form of money, but a faster, programmable way of moving money that already exists.

Inside the briefing

Six sections, ten pages

  1. 01

    What JPM Coin is

    A digital dollar deposit token for institutions: a tokenized bank deposit, your dollars on a faster rail.

  2. 02

    Not a public cryptocurrency

    How it differs from Bitcoin and public stablecoins in issuer, access, trading and backing.

  3. 03

    Backing and liability

    Every token is pegged 1:1 to a US dollar and recorded on JPMorgan's own balance sheet; its value does not float.

  4. 04

    Infrastructure

    The Kinexys platform and the Base Layer-2 network: always-on rails with no market close or batch cut-off.

  5. 05

    Purpose and risk mitigation

    Settlement in seconds, 24/7/365, removing the lag between execution and settlement and reducing counterparty risk.

  6. 06

    Access and compliance

    A closed network: institutional participants only, cleared through KYC verification and ongoing AML screening.
